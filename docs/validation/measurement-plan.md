# Measurement Plan

## Baseline

Collect at least two representative weeks before intervention where possible.

## Post-Retrofit

Collect comparable days after passive flow paths and controls are commissioned.

## Analysis

Compare:

- Temperature stratification.
- Time inside comfort band.
- Humidity excursions.
- CO2 levels.
- HVAC runtime proxy.
- Occupant feedback.

## Reporting Rule

Separate measured values, simulated model values, assumed inputs, and pilot targets in every chart.
