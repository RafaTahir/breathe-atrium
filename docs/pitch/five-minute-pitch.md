# Five-Minute Pitch

## Opening

Glass atriums are premium architectural spaces, but in tropical climates they can become heat reservoirs. BreatheAtrium turns the atrium from a heat trap into a measured breathing space.

## Selected IP

The selected UM IP uses solar-assisted natural ventilation for atrium cooling. Air enters low, crosses occupied zones, rises through a solar-heated chimney or upper exhaust path, and exits high. Optional water-wall support can be evaluated where humidity and maintenance conditions allow.

## Product

BreatheAtrium packages the IP into a retrofit:

- Airflow audit.
- Intake and exhaust path design.
- Sensor kit.
- BMS handoff.
- Monitoring dashboard.
- Pilot validation.

## Business

Start with tropical atriums where comfort is visible and energy burden matters: campuses, hospitals, malls, public buildings, and premium offices.

## Ask

Approve the measured pilot pathway: UM commercialization alignment, pilot site access, engineering partners, and seed support for instrumentation and retrofit design validation.
