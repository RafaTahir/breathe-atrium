# Scaling Potential

BreatheAtrium can scale because the problem is repeatable: tropical glass atriums trap heat, facilities teams feel the comfort complaints, and owners need a credible path that does not begin with full reconstruction.

The scaling strategy is to prove the retrofit logic locally, package it into a repeatable toolkit, and then distribute through certified partners.

## Malaysia First

Malaysia is the right first market because the climate, building typologies, and institutional access align with the problem. The first pilots should focus on atriums where heat build-up is obvious, facilities teams are engaged, and UM-linked credibility can support adoption.

The first Malaysian deployments should create:

- A baseline measurement playbook.
- Before/after dashboard evidence.
- A facility-manager approval template.
- A retrofit costing structure.
- A clear list of site conditions that make BreatheAtrium suitable or unsuitable.

The goal is a measured reference, not broad claims.

## Tropical Southeast Asia Next

The next expansion hypothesis is tropical Southeast Asia: dense cities, glass-heavy public buildings, malls, hotels, offices, universities, and transport hubs facing similar heat and comfort challenges.

This expansion should happen only after Malaysian pilots prove the core method. Each new country will require local building-code review, fire and smoke strategy alignment, climate assumptions, partner qualification, and facilities training.

## Portfolio Rollout Through Partners

BreatheAtrium becomes more valuable when sold across portfolios. REITs, campus operators, hotel groups, mall owners, and government estates often manage multiple atriums with similar operational pain.

The portfolio motion is:

```text
pilot one atrium -> verify results -> rank portfolio sites -> repeat through partners
```

This creates a practical route from one showcase pilot to multiple installations without pretending every atrium will perform the same way.

## Software / Design Toolkit

The product should evolve into a software-enabled design toolkit:

- Atrium intake and exhaust sizing guidance.
- Sensor placement templates.
- Passive-mode operating logic.
- BMS integration checklist.
- Pilot reporting format.
- ROI assumption calculator.
- Dashboard views for comfort, airflow trend, CO2, humidity, and cooling-energy proxy.

This toolkit makes BreatheAtrium easier to sell, install, and defend in Q&A. It also protects the product from being seen as a one-off construction concept.

## Certified Installer Network

Scaling should not depend on one internal installation team. BreatheAtrium should certify installers and delivery partners after the first pilots validate repeatable methods.

Certified partners can include MEP contractors, facade specialists, BMS integrators, ESCOs, and green-building consultants. The certification package should include design rules, installation QA, sensor commissioning, dashboard setup, safety constraints, and verification requirements.

The strategic advantage is leverage. BreatheAtrium owns the method, dashboard logic, pilot framework, partner certification, and IP-led product story while partners expand delivery capacity.
