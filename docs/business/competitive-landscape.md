# Competitive Landscape

BreatheAtrium is not positioned as a replacement for all air-conditioning. It competes against the default ways building teams respond to overheated atriums: more mechanical cooling, generic ventilation, decorative green features, or expensive redesign.

## Positioning

BreatheAtrium wins when the buyer wants more than a component. The offer combines passive architecture, sensor evidence, dashboard storytelling, and a practical commercial path.

The strongest differentiation is not simply "natural ventilation." It is the ability to turn an atrium comfort problem into an auditable, pilotable, and monitorable retrofit decision.
