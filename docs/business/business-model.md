# BreatheAtrium Business Model

BreatheAtrium commercializes solar-assisted natural ventilation as a measurable mixed-mode atrium retrofit. The business is built around a simple buyer journey: diagnose the heat problem, prove the intervention through a 90-day pilot, install the retrofit, and keep the site monitored.

## Revenue Streams

1. **Atrium audit**

   A paid diagnostic engagement covering site walk-through, heat-risk mapping, baseline sensor plan, ventilation path assessment, and retrofit concept. This creates a low-friction entry point and qualifies whether the atrium is suitable.

2. **90-day pilot**

   A paid proof package with baseline measurement, prototype or light retrofit works, monitored operation, and a verification report. Comfort improvement and cooling-burden reduction should be framed as **pilot targets**, not guaranteed savings.

3. **Retrofit installation**

   Full deployment of low-level intake, high-level exhaust path, solar-assisted chimney or upper exhaust strategy, optional water-wall or evaporative assist, controls, dashboard, and BMS handoff.

4. **Monitoring subscription**

   Monthly software and support revenue for dashboard access, reporting, anomaly review, seasonal tuning, and facilities-team decision support.

5. **Certified partner licensing**

   A scalable licensing model for qualified MEP, facade, ESCO, BMS, and green-building partners once repeatable pilot evidence and installation playbooks exist.

## Buyer

Primary buyers include building owners, facility managers, REITs, universities, government buildings, malls, hotels, and office towers with visible overheated atriums.

BreatheAtrium is strongest where the atrium is both an operational problem and a public-facing asset: too hot to ignore, too visible to treat as a back-of-house issue.

## Economic Buyer vs User vs Influencer

The **economic buyer** is typically the building owner, asset manager, REIT, campus administration, or government facilities budget holder. They care about capex justification, operational risk, tenant experience, ESG posture, and evidence.

The **user** is the facilities team operating the building day to day. They need a system that is understandable, maintainable, BMS-aware, and does not create new operational headaches.

The **influencers** include sustainability officers, architects, MEP consultants, occupants, tenants, researchers, and senior leadership. They shape urgency, credibility, and acceptance.

## Why Now

Atriums are expensive comfort zones in tropical buildings, and owners are under pressure to improve comfort without simply adding more mechanical cooling. Sensors, dashboards, and BMS integration now make passive strategies easier to measure, explain, and manage.

The market does not need another vague green feature. It needs retrofit concepts that can be piloted, instrumented, and turned into an operating decision.

## What Makes This Scalable

BreatheAtrium scales because the product is not a one-off architectural idea. It can become a repeatable package:

- A standard audit method.
- A sensor and measurement template.
- A design toolkit for intake, exhaust, and solar-assisted stack paths.
- A pilot report format that converts technical results into buyer language.
- A certified partner network for installation and support.

The core hypothesis is that many tropical atriums share the same pattern: heat accumulates high, comfort suffers low, and facilities teams lack a measurable passive-first retrofit option. BreatheAtrium turns that hypothesis into a commercial pilot path.
