# Judge Q&A

## Does this replace air-conditioning?

No. It is a mixed-mode retrofit that keeps HVAC available for humidity, peak load, and comfort assurance.

## What is the deep tech?

Solar-assisted natural ventilation translated into a measurable retrofit system: passive stack effect, controlled airflow path, optional evaporative support, sensors, and BMS integration.

## What is your first customer?

A tropical building owner with a visible glass atrium and a facilities team that cares about comfort, energy, and ESG: campus, hospital, mall, public building, or premium office.

## What are the biggest risks?

Site variability, humidity, fire and smoke strategy, maintenance burden, and comfort expectations.

## How do you handle IP?

Confirm current status, ownership, and licensing route with UM before implying exclusivity.
