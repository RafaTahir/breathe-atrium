BreatheAtrium prototype guide

Five-minute demo flow

Use this guide when mentors or judges scan the demo hub and want to understand the prototype quickly.

  1. Overview: explain the UM solar-assisted natural ventilation IP and the mixed-mode retrofit position.
  2. Live Demo: show simulated or CSV playback data, then switch between Dead Atrium and BreatheAtrium Mode.
  3. Design Engine: adjust atrium height, floor area, glazing, occupancy, and discomfort severity.
  4. ROI Calculator: show ranges, assumptions, and pilot targets without claiming measured savings.
  5. Pilot Report: close with the 90-day validation path and decision gate.

Firmware path: ESP32 or simple gateway streams newline-delimited JSON into the dashboard when hardware is available. The pitch demo remains reliable through simulated and CSV playback modes.
