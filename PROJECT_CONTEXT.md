# Project Context

## Project

**Name:** BreatheAtrium  
**Competition:** National Deep Tech Challenge  
**Selected IP:** Universiti Malaya, "A Passive Cooling System For An Atrium Of A Building By Using Solar-Assisted Natural Ventilation" (PI 2016700745)

## Core Pitch

BreatheAtrium turns overheated glass atriums into measurable, lower-energy breathing spaces. It uses UM's solar-assisted natural ventilation IP as the passive cooling engine:

- Low-level air intake.
- Cross-flow through occupied zones.
- Solar-heated chimney or upper exhaust path.
- High-level hot-air release.
- Optional water-wall or evaporative cooling.
- Sensors.
- BMS integration.
- Monitoring dashboard.

## Positioning

BreatheAtrium is a mixed-mode atrium comfort and energy retrofit. It reduces heat build-up, improves comfort hours where site conditions allow, and lowers cooling burden. It does not replace all air-conditioning.

## Design Philosophy

Cinematic tropical biophilic futurism. Premium, calm, architectural, clear. Use large type, minimal text, glassmorphism panels, deep teal, warm solar gold, off-white, and subtle airflow ribbons. The deck and app should feel like Apple meets high-end sustainable architecture.
